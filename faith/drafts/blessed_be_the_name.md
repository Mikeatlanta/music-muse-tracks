# Blessed Be the Name: lyric drafts & critique
Story: Job 1–2. Satan says Job only fears God because of the hedge around him. He loses the oxen, donkeys, sheep, camels and servants (Sabeans, fire from heaven, Chaldeans), then a wind collapses the house on his ten children. Then come boils, ashes and a potsherd, and his wife says "curse God and die". Job answers "The Lord gave, and the Lord has taken away; blessed be the name of the Lord", and later "I know that my Redeemer lives" (Job 19:25).

## Draft 1
[Verse 1]
I went before the throne and I said take down the hedge
I'll push your servant Job until he's falling off the edge
I took away his cattle and I burned up all his sheep
I killed his sons and daughters and I made the poor man weep
[Chorus]
But he never cursed the Lord
He never cursed the Lord
No matter what I tried
He never cursed the Lord
## Critique 1
- Hook: "he never cursed the Lord" is a negative and hard to chant with energy. The real, famous line is "blessed be the name", so use that as the title and hook.
- Theology: "I killed his sons and daughters" is too graphic and puts the devil at the center. Imply it with the wind and the house, as the text does, and keep God sovereign (the devil had to ask permission).
- Imagery: "falling off the edge" and "made the poor man weep" are filler.
- Meter: 15–16 syllable lines again, too long.

## Draft 2
[Intro]
(Numbers-station voice) Request to the throne... permission granted... the hedge is down
[Verse 1]
I said take down the hedge and he'll curse You to Your face
I sent Sabeans for the oxen, fire on the flocks in his place
[Pre-Chorus]
I took it all
I waited for the fall
[Chorus]
Blessed be the Name
He said it through the flame
Blessed be the Name
## Critique 2
- Hook: "Blessed be the Name" is instantly recognizable and chantable. It needs a 4-line chorus with the devil's loss stated clearly.
- Pre-chorus "I took it all / I waited for the fall" is strong. Keep it.
- "fire on the flocks in his place" doesn't scan, and V1 needs the wind and house detail.
- V2 should cover the boils, ashes, the potsherd and his wife's "curse God and die", the most recognizable details.
- Bridge: use the "Redeemer lives" line, the climax of Job's faith.

## Draft 3
[Chorus]
Blessed be the Name
I took it all, he stayed the same
The Lord gave and the Lord took away
Blessed be the Name
[Verse 2]
I covered him in boils from his head down to his feet
He sat in the ashes with a shard of broken pottery
His wife said curse your God and die
## Critique 3
- Chorus: quoting "the Lord gave and the Lord took away" anchors the story, but the chorus never says the devil lost. Change line 2 to "I lost the bet, he stayed the same"? "Bet" trivializes it. Use "I took it all, I lost the game"? Also cheap. Best: "I took his all, he blessed again". No, "Name/same" is the strongest rhyme. Final: line 2 "I took it all, he stayed the same" and line 4 "Blessed be the Name, and I'm put to shame" (devil defeated, rhymes).
- V2: "shard of broken pottery" is clunky. Use "scraping with a shard in the heat".
- The ending of the story matters: God restores Job double (Job 42). Put it in the outro.

## Final (Draft 4)
Biggest improvement: the hook moved from the negative "he never cursed the Lord" to Job's actual words "Blessed be the Name", and the devil's defeat ("I'm put to shame") is now built into the chorus. Graphic deaths became the biblical wind-and-house image.

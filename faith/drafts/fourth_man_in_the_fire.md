# Fourth Man in the Fire: lyric drafts & critique
Story: Daniel 3. King Nebuchadnezzar sets up a golden image 90 feet (60 cubits) tall on the plain of Dura. When the horn, flute, harp and pipes sound, everyone must bow. Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego refuse ("even if He does not"). The furnace is heated seven times hotter, and the soldiers who throw them in are killed by the heat. The king sees four men walking loose in the fire, "the fourth like a son of the gods". They come out with not a hair singed and no smell of smoke.

## Draft 1
[Verse 1]
I whispered to the king, build an idol made of gold
Make the people bow down low or they'll be burned up, so I told
Three young men refused to kneel, they wouldn't do as told
So we threw them in the furnace and we turned the heat up bold
[Chorus]
But there was a fourth man in the fire
Walking with them through the flames
Fourth man in the fire
And they came out the same
## Critique 1
- Rhymes: gold/told/told/bold is lazy. "So I told" and "turned the heat up bold" are filler.
- Hook: "Fourth man in the fire" is the right title and instantly recognizable. Cut "But there was" so it's chantable.
- Missing the iconic details: the names, the music signal, "seven times hotter", "not a hair singed", "no smell of smoke".
- No pre-chorus; chorus too late.

## Draft 2
[Intro]
(Numbers-station voice) Horn... flute... harp... all bow... all bow...
[Verse 1]
Ninety feet of gold on the plain of Dura
Every knee in Babylon hit the dirt for the ruler
[Pre-Chorus]
Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego
Three heads up in a kneeling row
[Chorus]
Fourth man in the fire
Seven times hotter, still no smoke
Fourth man in the fire
## Critique 2
- Intro: the music-signal countdown fits the numbers-station sound perfectly. Keep.
- The pre-chorus naming the three is the most recognizable moment, and the rhyme works. Keep.
- "Dura / ruler" is a slant rhyme, but it's awkward to chant. "Every knee" echoes Checkmate's bridge, so avoid it.
- Chorus needs 4 tight lines: the fourth man, seven times hotter, not a hair, and the devil's defeat.

## Draft 3
[Verse 1]
Ninety feet of gold on the plain of Dura shining
Pipes and harps the signal and the whole world lining
[Chorus]
Fourth man in the fire
Seven times hotter, I fed the flame
Not a hair, not a thread, not the smell of smoke
Fourth man in the fire
[Verse 2]
The guards that threw them in were the ones who burned
## Critique 3
- Chorus: "not a hair, not a thread, not the smell of smoke" is the best line, with concrete details from Daniel 3:27. But "flame / smoke" don't rhyme. Fix: line 2 "Seven times hotter, still they walk", line 4 "Fourth man in the fire, and the furnace talk"? Weak. Better: rhyme line 2 with line 3: "I stoked it seven times, I stoked it till it broke / not a hair, not a thread, not the smell of smoke".
- V1 "shining/lining" is filler. Rewrite as plain, concrete couplets.
- V2: the king leaping up ("Did we not cast three men bound?") is the reveal and should land there.
- Bridge: "even if He does not" is their famous faith line. Use it as a pitched-down spoken quote.

## Final (Draft 4)
Biggest improvement: the chorus became a concrete, recognizable chant (seven times / not a hair / smell of smoke) with a clean broke/smoke rhyme. The names went into the pre-chorus, and their "even if He does not" line became the bridge.
```
[Intro]
(Numbers-station voice, distorted) Horn... flute... harp... pipe... all bow... all bow...

[Verse 1]
(Growled)
Ninety feet of gold on the plain of Dura
I sold the king a mirror and I made his idol truer
When the music hit the air, all of Babylon went low
But three heads stayed up, like they didn't even know

[Pre-Chorus]
Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego
Three heads up in a kneeling row

[Chorus]
(Chant, gang vocals)
Fourth man in the fire
I stoked it seven times, I stoked it till it broke
Not a hair, not a thread, not the smell of smoke
Fourth man in the fire

[Verse 2]
(Growled)
Bound them up in robes and I threw them to the blaze
The soldiers at the door were the ones who burned that day
Then the king jumped up and he shouted at the wall
Didn't we throw three men? I see four men walking tall

[Pre-Chorus]
Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego
Loose in the flame where the bound men go

[Chorus]
Fourth man in the fire
I stoked it seven times, I stoked it till it broke
Not a hair, not a thread, not the smell of smoke
Fourth man in the fire

[Bridge]
(Pitched-down spoken voice) Our God is able... and even if He does not... we will not bow
(Growled, slower) I built the heat, I built the throne
But the Son of God was walking with His own

[Final Chorus]
(Full intensity, choir stab)
Fourth man in the fire
I stoked it seven times, I stoked it till it broke
Not a hair, not a thread, not the smell of smoke
Fourth man in the fire

[Outro]
(Numbers-station voice) Furnace... cold... three... four... walking out
(Static fades)
```

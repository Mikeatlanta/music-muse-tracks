# It Is Written: lyric drafts & critique
Story: the temptation of Jesus in the wilderness (Matthew 4 / Luke 4): stones to bread, the pinnacle of the temple, all the kingdoms of the world. Told by the devil; each attack is answered with "It is written."

## Draft 1
[Intro]
(Numbers-station voice) Forty... forty... forty days
[Verse 1]
I found Him in the desert where the hungry jackals cry
I offered Him a loaf of bread to make the hunger die
I showed Him every kingdom and the glitter of the gold
I told Him bow before me and the world is yours to hold
I took Him to the temple top and told Him now to fall
The angels they will catch you if you're who you say at all
[Chorus]
But He said it is written, it is written, it is written
And I fell, and I fell
It is written, it is written
I lost in the wilderness

## Critique 1
- Hook: "It is written" x3 is chantable, but the chorus has no rhyme or payoff, and "I lost in the wilderness" is flat.
- Structure: V1 has 6 long lines and no pre-chorus, so the chorus lands near 1:10, which is too late. All three temptations are used up in V1, leaving nothing for V2.
- Meter: lines run 14–16 syllables, too long for a growled chant at 128 BPM.
- Imagery: "hungry jackals cry" and "glitter of the gold" are clichés and filler.
- Theology: fine, but Jesus never speaks. The Word should be the weapon.

## Draft 2
[Intro]
(Pitched-down spoken) Forty days. No food. No witness.
[Verse 1]
Sun on the stone and His ribs showing through
I said turn it to bread, it's the least You can do
[Pre-Chorus]
He didn't flinch, He didn't break
He opened up His mouth and spake
[Chorus]
It is written, I fell
It is written, my hell
Every word was a blade
It is written, I'm slayed
[Verse 2]
Kingdoms in my hand, I said bow down, it's Yours
Off the temple wing, the angels guard the doors

## Critique 2
- Structure: a short V1 plus the pre-chorus brings the chorus in by about 0:40, and the temptations are now spread across the song. Good.
- Hook: "It is written, I fell" works. "my hell" and "I'm slayed" are forced rhymes, and "slayed" reads as slang, which sounds unintentionally comic.
- "spake" is an archaic word used only to make the rhyme. The pre-chorus is weak.
- V1 has only 2 lines; it needs 4 to match Checkmate's density.
- Jesus's three scripture answers should become a numbers-station bridge.

## Draft 3
[Verse 1]
Sun on the stone and His ribs showing through
I said turn it to bread, it's the least You can do
Forty nights hungry, no army, no throne
But He fed on a Word that I'd never known
[Pre-Chorus]
I came with a lie
He came with a line
[Chorus]
It is written, I lose
Every trap that I choose
It is written, I fell
Every lie I could sell

## Critique 3
- Pre-chorus: "I came with a lie / He came with a line" is tight and sets up the hook. Keep it.
- Chorus: "Every lie I could sell" is vague. The chorus needs a concrete image tied to the story (bread, stone, crown) and a clear "He wins" ending, with the title opening and closing it.
- V2 still needs the kingdoms and the temple pinnacle with clean rhymes (crowns/down, wall/fall).
- Bridge: have the numbers-station voice read the three replies ("not by bread alone", "worship God alone", "do not test the Lord"), then a growled confession.
- Outro: use a callback that doesn't reuse Checkmate's "signal lost".

## Final (Draft 4)
Biggest improvement: the chorus went from a repeated phrase to a 4-line hook that names the story ("bread from a stone") and ends on "He wins"; Jesus's actual scripture replies became the bridge.

```
[Intro]
(Pitched-down spoken voice) Forty days. No bread. No witness.
(Numbers-station voice, distorted) Four... zero... four... zero...

[Verse 1]
(Growled)

[Pre-Chorus]
I came with a lie
He came with a line

[Chorus]
(Chant, gang vocals)
It is written, I lose
Every trap that I choose
Bread from a stone, crown from a sin
It is written, He wins

[Verse 2]
(Growled)
I showed Him the kingdoms, the towers, the crowns
I said one little bow and I'll hand them all down
I dared Him to leap from the temple's high wall
He would not test the Father, so I watched myself fall

[Pre-Chorus]
I came with a lie
He came with a line

[Chorus]
It is written, I lose
Every trap that I choose
Bread from a stone, crown from a sin
It is written, He wins

[Bridge]
(Numbers-station voice) Message one: not by bread alone
(Numbers-station voice) Message two: worship God alone
(Numbers-station voice) Message three: do not test the Lord
(Growled, slower) Three words, three wounds, I fled before the sword

[Final Chorus]
(Full intensity, choir stab)
It is written, I lose
Every trap that I choose
Bread from a stone, crown from a sin
It is written, He wins

[Outro]
(Distorted whisper) It is written... it is written...
(Static) End of transmission
```
